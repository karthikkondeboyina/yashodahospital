Help & Support

How do I book an appointment?

Click "Talk to AI Assistant" or use "Book Appointment" in the header. Our system checks real-time doctor availability and confirms your slot instantly with an email confirmation.

Can an appointment slot be double-booked?

No. CareConnect AI uses database-level ACID transactions and strict uniqueness constraints. Once a slot is taken by a patient, it is immediately unavailable to all other patients.

Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?

Yes, you can contact our FrontDesk at +91 95132 62681, or our FrontDesk Admin can view and update appointments directly in the Admin Portal.

Emergency Contact

For emergencies, call 105910 or 108 immediately. Do not use the appointment assistant for emergencies.
